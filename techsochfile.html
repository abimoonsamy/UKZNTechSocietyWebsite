    For you, not for members. Only reachable from the footer link.

    If a row above is red

    1. sheet_id missing: open TCsite.html in a text editor, find SHEET_URL_OR_ID near the top, paste your sheet's full web address between the quotes. The whole URL is fine, it gets cleaned automatically.
    2. Cannot reach sheet: in Google Sheets click Share, set General access to Anyone with the link, role Viewer.
    3. Tab not found: the tabs must be named exactly Site, Campuses, About, Benefits, Gallery, Events, Team.
    4. join_form not set: Google Form > Send > < > embed > copy the link inside src="..." into GOOGLE_FORM_URL.

    Opening a new campus

    Add a row to the Campuses tab: name, a hex colour like #A855F7, a short code, and active. It appears in the hero ring, the filters, the calendar and the team cards immediately. Set the status to anything other than active to hide a campus you have not opened yet.

    Content lives in the Google Sheet online. A spreadsheet saved on your laptop is not connected to anything.
